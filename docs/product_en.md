# Guanlan Wealth OS

## Product Overview

Guanlan Wealth OS is an intelligent personal wealth management and growth analysis system designed for individual users.

By integrating personal balance sheets, cash flow analysis, financial data processing, and artificial intelligence technologies, Guanlan helps users understand their financial status, risk profile, and future development paths.

## Product Vision

Enable ordinary people to understand and manage their wealth like business managers.

## Core Features

### 1. Personal Balance Sheet

Record and analyze:

- Assets
- Liabilities
- Income
- Expenses
- Net Worth


### 2. Cash Flow Analysis

Analyze income and expenses to help users understand their financial flows, spending patterns, and potential problems.


### 3. Wealth Growth Analysis

Analyze personal wealth development stages based on age, career stage, and historical data, providing personalized insights.


### 4. Financial Risk Assessment

Evaluate potential financial risks:

- Debt Risk
- Cash Flow Risk
- Investment Risk


### 5. Financial Knowledge System

Use historical financial events, market changes, and real-world cases to help users understand how financial systems work.


## Future Development

Future versions will include:

- AI Wealth Assistant
- Personal Wealth Simulation System
- Investment Learning System
- Data-driven Wealth Analysis


## Target Users

- University Students
- Young Professionals
- Solo Entrepreneurs
- Individuals interested in personal wealth management
